I'm David, a software engineer based in Scotland.

At work I build software for a living. At home I can't leave things alone: the house runs on
[Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/), with solar panels, a home battery, an EV charger, smart heating
in every room and far more lights than any family needs to control from a phone.

This site is where I write those projects up. Most of them start with "I wonder if I could…" and end several
evenings later with a dashboard.

## What you'll find here

- **Home automation**: Home Assistant integrations, automations and dashboards.
- **Energy**: getting the most out of solar, a battery and a cheap overnight tariff.
- **3D and rendering**: Blender as a tool for things that aren't films or games.
